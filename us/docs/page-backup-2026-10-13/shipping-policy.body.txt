<p>We want your order to arrive quickly, safely and exactly as described. Here's everything you need to know about how we ship.</p>
<h2>Processing Time</h2>
<p>Orders are typically processed within <strong>1–2 business days</strong> of payment confirmation. You will receive a confirmation email once your order has been dispatched.</p>
<h2>Estimated Delivery Times</h2>
<p>Delivery times vary depending on the product and your location. As a general guide:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>United States:</strong> 5–12 business days</li>
<li>
<strong>International:</strong> 10–20 business days</li>
</ul>
<p>These are estimates only. Occasional delays may occur due to high demand, carrier capacity, or customs processing for international orders.</p>
<h2>Shipping Costs</h2>
<p>We offer free standard shipping on every order, with no minimum purchase required. You will not be charged for shipping at checkout.</p>
<h2>Order Tracking</h2>
<p>Once your order has shipped, you will receive a tracking number by email. Use this to monitor your delivery through the carrier's website.</p>
<h2>Lost or Delayed Orders</h2>
<p>If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated timeframe, please contact us at <a href="mailto:support@trenzora.com">support@trenzora.com</a> and we will investigate promptly.</p>
<h2>Incorrect Address</h2>
<p>Please ensure your delivery address is correct at checkout. We are unable to redirect parcels once they have been dispatched. If you notice an error, contact us immediately after placing your order.</p>
<p>If you have any questions about your shipment, please don't hesitate to get in touch.</p>
