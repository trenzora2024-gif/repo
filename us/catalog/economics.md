## Assumptions

- Payment: 3% + $0.30 per order (Shopify Payments, Basic plan).
- Returns/refunds allowance: 5% of price. Discount allowance: 2%.
- Shipping: $0 (free-shipping Doba listings). Confirm per product; add any shipping to the CSV.
- Target contribution before ads: ≥25%. Break-even ROAS = price ÷ contribution.
- Scenarios where Doba cost isn’t verified: cost = 60% / 72% / 85% of the lowest public retail price.
