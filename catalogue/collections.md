# Shopify collections (V1 launch)

Create the V1 collections as **smart collections** in Shopify Admin so products join automatically via tags. Counts are V1 launch products only.

| Handle | Title | Rule | V1 products | Release |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `all` | All Products | Built-in Shopify collection | 24 | V1: built in (do not create) |
| `mumbai-made` | Mumbai Made | Product tag equals col:mumbai-made | 12 | V1: create |
| `drops` | Drops | Product tag equals col:drops | 24 | V1: create |
| `personalize` | Personalize | Product tag equals col:personalize | 0 | V2: do not create in V1 |
| `gifts` | Gifts | Product tag equals col:gifts | 18 | V1: create |
| `trending` | Trending | Product tag equals col:trending (editorial until sales data exists) | 12 | V1: create |

Collection descriptions (paste into Shopify):

- **all** — Every Trenzora original — oversized tees, totes and 20oz tumblers, made to order in India.
- **mumbai-made** — Every Trenzora design for Mumbai: Mumbai Made, Local Legend, Bombay Coffee Club and माझी मुंबई, on tees, totes and tumblers.
- **drops** — Original Trenzora designs, released in drops. New designs land every week.
- **gifts** — Gifts with personality: for besties, colleagues, coffee people, pet parents and anyone who misses Mumbai.
- **trending** — What people with personality are picking right now.
