# Shopify collections (launch)

Create these as **smart collections** in Shopify Admin so products join automatically via tags.

| Handle | Title | Rule | Products |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `all` | All Products | Built-in Shopify collection | 30 |
| `mumbai-made` | Mumbai Made | Product tag equals col:mumbai-made | 3 |
| `drops` | Drops | Product tag equals col:drops | 30 |
| `personalize` | Personalize | Product tag equals col:personalize | 6 |
| `gifts` | Gifts | Product tag equals col:gifts | 24 |
| `trending` | Trending | Product tag equals col:trending (editorial until sales data exists) | 12 |

Collection descriptions (paste into Shopify):

- **all** — Every Trenzora original — oversized tees, totes and 20oz tumblers, made to order in India.
- **mumbai-made** — The first Trenzora drop. A love letter to Mumbai on a tee, a tote and a tumbler.
- **drops** — Original Trenzora designs, released in drops. New designs land every week.
- **personalize** — Designs made to carry your names, dates and words. Originals available now; personal text coming soon.
- **gifts** — Gifts with personality — for besties, couples, colleagues, pet parents and anyone who misses home.
- **trending** — What people with personality are picking right now.
